Delete your account

Last updated: 25 November 2026

You can delete your Payday Planner account and everything in it at any time. Here is how.

Delete it yourself

  1. Open Payday Planner (the app or mypayplanner.com) and sign in.
  2. Open Settings, then Account.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

Or ask us to do it

If you cannot sign in, email hello@mypayplanner.com from the email address on the account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, your email address and your sign-in details.
  • Your whole planner: paychecks, bills, debts, goals, budgets, spending and settings.
  • Plan and subscription records and access code redemptions linked to the account.

What may remain

  • Copies in our providers’ backups and logs, for a limited time until they are overwritten.
  • Limited purchase and tax records, if the law requires us to keep them.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Cancel it in that store’s subscription settings so you are not charged again.

Want a copy of your data first? Use Export backup in Settings before you delete.
