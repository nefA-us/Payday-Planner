Payday Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Payday Planner (“we”, “us”) is a budgeting app. This policy covers our website and our Android and iPhone apps. It explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Email hello@mypayplanner.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored in scrambled form and we cannot read them. You may also give us a name.
  • Budget information you enter: paychecks, bills, debts, goals, budgets, spending, balances and settings. You choose what to enter.
  • Error reports: if the app hits a problem while you are signed in, it may send us the error message, the page, the app version, your browser type and your account ID so we can fix it. Reports never include your budget entries. You can turn them off in Settings under Help and about.
  • Plan and subscription details: which plan you have (Free, Plus or Together), whether it is active, when it ends, and whether access was given to you by us or by an access code you entered. If you subscribe, the app store tells us your subscription status. We never see your card number or payment details.
  • Basic technical data: your device and browser type, and standard server logs such as IP address and time of visit, which our providers keep for security and reliability.

We do not connect to your bank, and we never ask for your bank login, Social Security number or account numbers. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us.

Receipt photos: if you use the receipt feature, the photo is read on your own device to find the total. It is not uploaded or saved. Only the amount, date and note you keep are stored.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your plan, calendar and reminders, and sync your data between your devices.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent misuse.
  • To send service emails, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • To find and fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your budget data for advertising.

We share information only with companies that help us run the app, and only as needed:

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety. If the business is ever sold or merged, we will tell you first, and the promises in this policy will continue to apply.

Security and how long we keep data

Your data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with our database provider. Access rules are set so each person can read only their own planner. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your planner is removed. Copies may remain in our providers’ backups and logs for a limited time before they are overwritten. We may keep limited purchase and tax records for as long as the law requires. Subscriptions bought through an app store are not cancelled by deleting your account, so cancel them in the store as well.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export: use Export backup in Settings to download a copy of your planner.
  • Correct: you can edit everything you entered in the app.
  • Delete: in Settings, choose Delete my account to remove your account and all your data. You can also email us and we will do it for you. Details are on our Delete your account page.
  • Reminders: you can turn notifications on or off in the app and in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, such as California or the European Union, you may have extra rights, including to know what we hold, to object to certain uses, and to complain to a regulator. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

Payday Planner is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If we make important changes to this policy, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact: Payday Planner, hello@mypayplanner.com.
